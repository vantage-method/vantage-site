FAQ

What is Vantage Agent?

An AI operator for your business’s marketing. You talk to it in the Vantage Agent app, and it works in your own accounts: it runs ads, builds and publishes pages, sends follow-ups and reports back. It asks before anything that spends money or goes out under your name.

What happens when I sign up?

Your agent is built within minutes. It says hello, reviews your website, and interviews you about your business, a few questions at a time. Everything you tell it goes into your business profile, which you can see and edit. It also builds you a practice page so you can see what it can do.

What are the first 3 days “on us”?

For your first 3 days your agent runs on our account while it learns your business. After that it runs on your own Claude subscription, about $100 a month paid directly to Anthropic. Connecting it takes about two minutes from your account page or the Mac app, and your agent picks up right where it left off.

What can it connect to?

Google (Analytics, Search Console and Ads), Meta, GoHighLevel, Mailchimp, Klaviyo, Dropbox, Stripe, Shopify, website builders like WordPress and Squarespace, and more. Most connect with a one-tap sign-in on your own account. Your real accounts unlock once your Claude subscription is connected.

Does it act without asking me?

Not on anything that matters. Spending money, emailing your list, publishing to your live site and similar steps wait for your yes. You can watch every step it takes in the app as it happens.

Where can I use it?

On iPhone, on Mac and in any web browser. It’s the same agent and the same conversation everywhere.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Premium is month to month. Manage or cancel your subscription anytime from your account page.

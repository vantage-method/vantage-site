Questions

Everything you’re wondering.

Is Vantage Method just another AI chatbot?

No. Vantage is configured around your company’s marketing ecosystem and built around specialized marketing workflows — strategy, ads, content, SEO, design, websites, email and booking — working together, not a single generic assistant.

Do I need marketing experience?

No. Vantage is useful for experienced marketers and for business owners with no marketing background. You bring the objective; Vantage helps orchestrate the execution.

Does Vantage replace my marketing team?

Think of Vantage as a force multiplier. It can reduce your dependence on outside vendors, accelerate the work, and give your existing team dramatically more capability.

What happens during onboarding?

We handle discovery, platform configuration, AI agent customization, brand training, account integrations and initial strategic setup — installing Vantage around your specific business, then training your team.

Why is there a setup fee?

Because the platform is configured around your individual company rather than delivered as generic software. That customization is what makes Vantage understand your market, offers and customers.

What integrations are supported?

Vantage connects to your key marketing accounts and platforms during onboarding. Your confirmed integrations are set up as part of your launch — we’ll walk through exactly which ones apply to you.

Can I cancel?

Annual memberships carry a 12‑month agreement. The month‑to‑month subscription gives you greater flexibility with no long‑term commitment.

Why are the last two months free on the annual plan?

Because we want to reward companies that commit to implementing Vantage as part of their long‑term marketing infrastructure.

Why Bethany Global

Don't just study missions. Do them.

Most schools teach you about the world. BGU puts you in it. Our students learn Scripture and strategy on campus, then step into real cross-cultural ministry — so what you believe gets tested and proven before you graduate.

1
Learn deeply. Biblically grounded academics built for the field, not just the exam.
2
Go for real. Hands-on cross-cultural ministry is built into the degree, not bolted on.
3
Be sent well. Graduate with a calling, a community, and a clear next step.

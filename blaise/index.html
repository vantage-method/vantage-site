Who I Am

Built for the leaders who need sales discipline, not more motivation.

I'm Blaise Foret, and I've spent the last 26+ years helping people develop the discipline, systems, and leadership it takes to actually achieve their goals — first from the stage as a motivational speaker, now from the trenches as Senior Vice President of The Leazer Group, an Integrity Marketing Company.

As founder of the Wake Up and Win Insurance Agency, I lead and develop 50+ agents in the life insurance industry — building the repeatable sales systems, daily operating rhythms, and leadership culture that drive consistent performance virtually and over the phone, at scale.

Before insurance, I spent 17 years on the speaking circuit — addressing audiences from 10 in a living room to 1,000 in an auditorium on motivation, leadership, business, and success. I've also authored two books, including an Amazon best-seller on leadership. What I bring to Vantage Method clients: the practical sales-process discipline and the leadership infrastructure to make those processes actually stick.

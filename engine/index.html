It really runs them. Connect your Meta account once and your agent builds the campaigns, launches them, checks performance on a schedule, shifts budget away from what's losing, and writes and posts new creative when a winner starts to fatigue. You approve the direction; it does the clicking.

Yes. It researches the keywords worth chasing, writes and publishes the pages, fixes titles, metadata and internal linking, cleans up the technical issues holding your site back, and watches Search Console so it can tell you what moved. Can it build me a website or a funnel?

Can it actually…

Yes. And yes.

Does it really run my ads, or just write them?

It really runs them. Connect your Meta account once and your agent builds the campaigns, launches them, checks performance on a schedule, shifts budget away from what's losing, and writes and posts new creative when a winner starts to fatigue. You approve the direction; it does the clicking.

Can it help me rank on Google?

Yes. It researches the keywords worth chasing, writes and publishes the pages, fixes titles, metadata and internal linking, cleans up the technical issues holding your site back, and watches Search Console so it can tell you what moved. SEO fails from inconsistency more than from ignorance — this is the part it's built for.

Can it build me a website or a funnel?

Yes — a real one, live. Describe the offer and you get a working page deployed to your hosting with the domain pointed and the code saved in your GitHub. Lead magnets, opt-in forms, thank-you pages and the follow-up email sequence included. Then ask for changes in plain English and they ship in minutes.

Will it email my list and follow up on leads?

Yes. It sends from your own domain, writes the sequences, and can pull your numbers back into the conversation so you can ask "which email actually got replies?" and get a real answer instead of a dashboard export.

Do I have to know anything technical?

No. You text it like you'd text a capable employee. On Base, setup is: subscribe, follow the two-minute walkthrough in your dashboard, scan a code with your phone — then connecting an account is signing in and clicking Allow. On Pro and Enterprise we do every technical part for you. Either way, everything after setup is a conversation.

How is this different from an AI chatbot?

Those give you words. This one has hands. It runs on a real computer of its own with your real logins, so it can deploy a site, launch a campaign, push code and run tomorrow morning's work without you. The chat window is where you ask — the work happens on a machine that never sleeps.

What does it cost?

Three ways in. $500/month for Base (in beta) — turnkey: you connect your accounts with one-tap sign-ins and your agent runs on your own Claude subscription. $1,000/month for Pro when you pay annually ($12,000 a year, launch build included), or $1,300 month to month plus a one-time $2,500 launch build — our team hand-builds and tests every connection, including custom API integrations, with guided onboarding. Enterprise is priced per company — agents across your teams, internal integrations and dedicated support; book a call and we'll scope it with you. Cancel anytime. Your Claude subscription (~$100/mo) is the one thing paid separately, straight to Anthropic.

What do I need on my end?

Three things: an iPhone, the logins for the accounts you want your agent working in, and your own Claude subscription — that's the AI your agent thinks with. On Base your dashboard walks you through connecting it in a couple of minutes; on Pro and Enterprise we set it up with you on your onboarding call. No Mac to leave running and nothing to install: we build and host the agent. Your keys go into your own agent's environment and are never shared with anyone else's — the details of what we do and don't store are on the privacy page.

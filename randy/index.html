Who I Am

Built for founders who think bigger.

I'm Randy Olsson — a serial entrepreneur and co-founder of Vantage Method. My career has been built at the intersection of vision and execution: taking ambitious concepts, architecting the revenue strategy, and translating them into businesses that ship product and serve customers at scale.

I started by building War Tribe Gear, which grew into a global leader in Jiu-Jitsu apparel and equipment, serving customers in over 100 countries. Today I lead Imagine Paradigm — designing and building luxury micro-resorts and short-term rental properties in the Montana mountains — and Cabin Craft ADU, which produces high-efficiency modular homes and accessory dwelling units.

Across e-commerce, real estate, hospitality, and consulting, my approach is the same: clarity of vision, ruthless focus on revenue, and the operational discipline to execute. At Vantage Method, I help founders cut through the noise and build companies that don't just generate revenue — they create real, lasting market position.
